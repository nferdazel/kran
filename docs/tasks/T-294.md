## Scope

### Part 1 - shell reflow at 360 and 200%

Symptoms, measured on this tree: the topbar puts a three-part business date (`label: value`) next to
the breadcrumb with no wrap, so at narrow widths it pushes the document wider than the viewport; the
off-canvas panel is `15rem` wide capped at `85vw`, so at 180 CSS px each nav label wraps and the menu
reads as four lines; `/app/ledger`'s `.kran-toolbar` (period `Select` + two buttons, `+page.svelte:264-280`)
has a min-content width of about 250px that neither wraps nor shrinks; `/app/loans` still reports
`pageOverflow` 124 at 360 in the T-288 log.

Fix direction, mechanism left to the implementer:

- The topbar may wrap the business date to a second row at narrow widths; it must stay visible and
  readable (the value is not hidden, only the arrangement changes).
- The panel nav is a vertical list at every width below 1024; labels do not wrap, so the menu is one
  line per link. Do not shrink the label font to fit.
- Toolbar controls shrink before the row overflows (`min-inline-size: 0` on the flex child that holds
  the select is the usual fix); buttons may wrap under the field, never off the edge.
- Any remaining page overflow on `/app/loans` and `/app/ledger` at 360/180 is found by measurement,
  not by guessing: the spec reports the element whose right edge exceeds the viewport.

### Part 2 - one tab stop on `/app/ledger`

Today the balances table is `<table role="grid" tabindex="0" aria-activedescendant=...>` with
`ledger-cell-{row}-{column}` ids and an Arrow/Home/End handler that moves a visual active cell
(`+page.svelte:168-215, 370-380`). This is D-116, and it is the only table in the app that does not
use the `DataTable` wrapper (`packages/ui/src/components/DataTable.svelte`, `role="region" tabindex="0"`
on `.kran-table-wrap`).

Target: the wrapper `.kran-table-wrap` carries `role="region"`, `tabindex="0"` and the aria-label;
the table goes back to a plain native table with no `tabindex`, no `role="grid"`, no
`aria-activedescendant`, no cell ids and no keydown handler. Arrow keys and Home/End scroll the
focused region natively. Rows, `th scope`, `<caption>` and the reading order are unchanged.

### Deliberately not in scope

- The `DataTable` component itself; T-294 only makes the ledger use its wrapper pattern.
- Any new DataTable feature (cell navigation, sticky columns).
- Shell redesign, sidebar width token changes, palette behaviour.
- Row density (T-293) and state copy (T-292).

## Acceptance

1. At viewport 360x800 and 180x400 (200% of a 360 device), DPR 1, light and dark, for `/app/ledger`
   and `/app/loans`: `document.documentElement.scrollWidth - clientWidth === 0`. Parent commit: 104
   (ledger) and 124 (loans) at 360, per `T-288-zoom.log`; 180 is measured in the new spec.
2. Topbar: the business date element's right edge is inside the topbar box at 360 and 180, its text
   is not clipped (`scrollWidth <= clientWidth`), and it stays visible (not `display: none`).
3. Drawer nav at 360 and 180 with the panel open: every nav link renders on one line
   (`scrollWidth <= clientWidth` and box height <= 1.6 x line height); the number of wrapped links is
   0. Parent: labels wrap until the menu is four lines.
4. `/app/ledger` toolbars: every direct child of `.kran-toolbar` has its right edge within the
   container at 360 and 180; the period `Select` is not wider than its container.
5. `/app/ledger` balances area has exactly one tab stop: one element with `tabindex="0"`, which is
   the `.kran-table-wrap` with `role="region"`; the table has no `tabindex`, no `role`, no
   `aria-activedescendant`; `rg -n 'ledger-cell-|aria-activedescendant|role="grid"' apps/web/src`
   returns 0.
6. Keyboard-only on `/app/ledger`: Tab from the last filter control focuses the region, the focus ring
   is visible and passes contrast in both themes, ArrowDown/ArrowRight/End scroll the region to the
   last row and last column, and Shift+Tab leaves the region. No keyboard trap, no focus loss.
7. Total focusable count on `/app/ledger` and `/app/loans` at 1440 is unchanged from the parent commit
   (the reflow adds no tab stop); the new spec prints the count per route.
8. axe 0 violations on `/app/ledger`, `/app/loans`, and `/app/dayclose` at 1440 and 360 in both
   themes; `./task check-fast` and `./task e2e -- ledger` green; `node tools/qa/a11y/keyboard-flows.mjs
   --config keyboard-app` reports no BLOCKED flow for the ledger.
