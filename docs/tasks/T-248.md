## Scope

Decide, per record class, three things: the enforcement mechanism, the parameter key that carries
the period, and whether the period is already settled. Periods that are not settled stay
`NEEDS-COMPLIANCE-REVIEW` and their purge task is not built.

Deliberately out of scope: writing the purge tasks (T-282), the manual anchor copy and its
disclosure (T-249), backup retention on the host (already decided, `docs/COMPLIANCE.md` §6).

## The finding: the mechanism is not uniform

Retention cannot be one sweep with one number, because two of these classes are physically unable
to lose rows.

| Class | Where | Purge possible today | Constraint that decides it | Parameter key (proposed) | Period |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Financial postings | `ledger.journal`, `ledger.journal_line`, module subledgers | No | Immutable by trigger; a correction is a reversing journal, never a delete | none - permanent | Settled: permanent |
| Audit log | `platform.audit_log`, host anchor file | No | `audit_log_no_change_trg` refuses UPDATE/DELETE and `audit_log_no_purge_trg` refuses TRUNCATE; a prefix delete also breaks `platform.audit_verify()` `link_ok` on the first surviving row | `platform.audit.retention_years` | Unsigned |
| KYC and identity | `party` | No | Encrypted and masked; period runs from membership life plus a tail | `party.retention_years_after_exit` | Unsigned |
| Teller sessions and cash | `teller` | No | Cash-control evidence; same weight as the cash it counts | `teller.retention_years` | Unsigned |
| Outbox | `platform.outbox` | Yes, by `status` and age | Delivery evidence only, no chain, nothing legal attaches to it | `platform.outbox.retention_days` | Unsigned |
| Inbox | `platform.inbox` | Yes, by `received_at` | De-dup rows only; losing one re-delivers an event, nothing worse | `platform.inbox.retention_days` | Unsigned |
| Idempotency responses | `platform.idempotency_key` | Yes, sweep exists | D-175: 24h default, 30 days hard cap, may hold member data | `platform.idempotency.ttl_hours` | Settled |
| Sessions and login events | `platform.session` | Yes, `purgeExpiredSessions` | Driven by `session_idle_minutes` / `session_absolute_minutes`, not by retention | none | Settled |
| Backups | Host filesystem | Host cron | 7 daily / 28 weekly / 84 monthly, same host, unencrypted | none | Settled, host not repo |

So five classes need a signed number (audit, party, teller, outbox, inbox). The other four are
already settled and must not be reopened: financial postings stay permanent, idempotency keeps the
D-175 cap, sessions are governed by expiry not retention, backups are a host policy.

Two things follow from the schema and belong in T-282, not here:

1. **The audit class cannot be purged at all under the current guards.** Purging it would mean
   dropping an immutable trigger and re-baselining the chain, which is a weakening of a guard and
   needs its own decision. The honest options are "keep, and bound growth by an archive-and-rotate
   task with a recorded chain baseline" or "keep forever for the pilot". A purge task here is not a
   smaller change, it is a different one.
2. **`platform.outbox` and `platform.inbox` have no index that supports an age delete.**
   `outbox_dispatch_idx` is `(status, available_at, seq)` and `inbox` is keyed
   `(consumer, event_id)`. An age-based purge scans. T-282 needs an index for each, and that is a
   schema change, which is why T-282 is `full`.

## What must be signed

The compliance owner signs a number for these five, or explicitly rules "keep forever for the
pilot". Nothing else in the list is open:

1. `platform.audit.retention_years` - and with it, whether growth is bounded by an archive task.
2. `party.retention_years_after_exit` - KYC and identity after a member leaves.
3. `teller.retention_years` - cash-control evidence.
4. `platform.outbox.retention_days` - after `DONE`/`DEAD`.
5. `platform.inbox.retention_days`.

## Proposed decision (D-xxx, awaiting signature)

Split the five rather than sign them in one block, because only two of them can actually be purged
without touching a guard:

- **Evidence classes - audit, party, teller: keep, no purge, for the pilot.** They are the three
  that carry legal weight and the three that the schema already refuses to delete. Signing a period
  shorter than the pilot buys nothing and forces the guard-dropping question above. Record the
  growth as an operational limit in `docs/ACCEPTED-LIMITS.md` and let T-249's daily anchor be the
  bound on the audit class.
- **Delivery classes - outbox, inbox: sign a day count.** These are the only two with zero
  evidentiary value, no chain and no guard. Recommended shape `30` days after `DONE`/`DEAD` for
  outbox and `30` days for inbox, matching the idempotency cap so one sweep can cover all three,
  tagged `NEEDS-COMPLIANCE-REVIEW` until the owner confirms it. This is the part of the table where
  a wrong number costs disk, not evidence.

Under this split T-282 shrinks to outbox and inbox purge plus the two indexes, and the audit class
gets a separate archive task that is not a purge.

## Acceptance

1. Every class in the table above has a named mechanism, a parameter key or an explicit "no key",
   and a period status of `settled` or `NEEDS-COMPLIANCE-REVIEW`. A class with a period and no key,
   or a key and no mechanism, fails this.
2. No period in this task is invented. Every unsigned number appears as `NEEDS-COMPLIANCE-REVIEW`
   with an owner, and T-282 stays `BLOCKED` until all five are signed.
3. The settled four (financial, idempotency, sessions, backups) are recorded as settled with the
   decision that settled them, so a later task cannot quietly reopen them.
