## Scope

One shared `MoneyInput` on top of the T-286 `Input` semantics: label carries the currency once
("Nominal (IDR)"), `inputmode="decimal"`, thousand separators in the editable value, `parseMoneyInput`
at submit only, error through `aria-describedby` and `aria-invalid`, no per-keystroke live region.
Migrate the four call sites above.

Not in scope: `Money` display, `AmountSummary`, ledger or totalling rules, DataTable money columns
(T-289), and any change to the parse contract without its own decision.

## Acceptance

- On the parent commit the new `components.test.ts` cases fail: no `MoneyInput` export exists.
- A bad amount ("1,2,3") leaves the raw string untouched, shows the parse error, sets
  `aria-invalid="true"` and points `aria-describedby` at an id that exists in the DOM.
- A valid amount ("1.234.567,89") produces `Money` with 2 dp and one separator; the label states the
  currency exactly once.
- The e2e negative path on the first migrated screen fails when the focus move is removed (the T-286
  lesson: a green local gate did not catch a dead focus move).
