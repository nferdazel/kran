## Scope

Current labels, read from the tree (id / en):

| Key | Today (id) | Today (en) | Becomes (id) | Becomes (en) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `loans_col_principal` | Pokok | Principal | Pokok (IDR) | Principal (IDR) |
| `loans_detail_principal` | Pokok akad | Agreed principal | Pokok akad (IDR) | Agreed principal (IDR) |
| `loans_detail_outstanding` | Sisa pokok | Outstanding principal | Sisa pokok (IDR) | Outstanding (IDR) |
| `loans_detail_interest_overdue` | Tunggakan bunga | Overdue interest | Tunggakan bunga (IDR) | Overdue interest (IDR) |
| `loans_detail_penalty` | Tunggakan denda | Overdue penalty | Tunggakan denda (IDR) | Overdue penalty (IDR) |
| `loans_schedule_principal` | Pokok | Principal | Pokok (IDR) | Principal (IDR) |
| `loans_schedule_interest` | Bunga | Interest | Bunga (IDR) | Interest (IDR) |
| `loans_schedule_penalty` | Denda | Penalty | Denda (IDR) | Penalty (IDR) |
| `loans_schedule_paid` | Pokok dibayar | Principal paid | Pokok dibayar (IDR) | Principal paid (IDR) |

`en` `loans_detail_outstanding` shortens to "Outstanding (IDR)" because "Outstanding principal
(IDR)" is 27 characters and the label budget is 24 (`docs/design/04-a11y-and-copy.md:269`). The id
value keeps "pokok".

The facts block on `/app/loans/[loanId]` is a `<dl>`, not a table. It is in scope because each `dt`
is the only label for one money value, which is exactly the MoneyInput rule quoted above; leaving
four unlabelled rupiah amounts next to four labelled columns would be the same bug with a smaller
diff.

**Out of scope**

- The money inputs in the action panels (disburse, payment, recovery) and their previews: their
  labels are `loans_field_recovery_principal` and siblings, and they are form fields, not columns.
  They also lack the unit today; that is a separate copy task, not silently folded in here.
- `formatMoney`, `packages/ui/src/money.ts` and `Money.svelte`: untouched, proven by an empty diff.
- Per-cell currency of any kind; cells stay digits and separators only.
- Print/slip/statement headers, ledger, teller, vault, products, deposits: already carry their unit
  or belong to another ticket.
- T-293 density, T-292 empty copy.
